# Privacy

The author of Dynamic Audio collects no personal data. There is no account, no analytics, no ads and no server that receives anything from the app.

## What stays on your Mac

- System audio: the spectrum is computed as the sound plays, nothing is recorded.
- The camera image in the Mirror tab.
- Clipboard history, files on the shelf, calendar events.
- Claude Code and Codex session details, listening statistics and your settings.

## What the app sends

The app talks to these services directly from your Mac. The author does not see these requests.

| Feature | Service | What it sends | When |
|---|---|---|---|
| Lyrics | LRCLIB, NetEase Cloud Music, Yandex Music if you signed in | Track title and artist | On from the start, while music plays |
| Updates | GitHub | A request for the latest version | On from the start, every few hours |
| Weather | Open-Meteo | The city name you entered | After you enter a city |
| Sharp cover on the Stage | Yandex Music if you signed in, otherwise Apple (iTunes Search) | Track title and artist | While the Stage you turned on is open and the player's cover is small |
| Yandex Music | Yandex | Requests with your token, which is kept in the Keychain | After you sign in |

Each of these can be switched off in Settings.

## Supporting the author

If you support the author, the payment and your details are handled by Tribute, under its privacy policy. The author sees only what Tribute shows: the amount and your message, if you left one. You can hide your account by sending the donation anonymously.
