# Support

Dynamic Audio is free and made by one person. There is no support contract or promised response time, but every issue gets read.

## Something does not work

Check [If it does not open](README.md#if-it-does-not-open) first. If that does not help, open a [bug report](https://github.com/5DHelLD5/dynamic-audio-releases/issues/new?template=bug_report.yml) with your Dynamic Audio version (Settings → About), your macOS version and the steps that lead to the problem.

## An idea

Open a [feature request](https://github.com/5DHelLD5/dynamic-audio-releases/issues/new?template=feature_request.yml). Describe the problem you want solved, not only the solution you have in mind.

## A security issue

Keep it out of public issues. [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) explains how to report it privately.

## Documents

- [Privacy](PRIVACY.md): what leaves your Mac and when

## Support the author

Dynamic Audio is free. If you want to thank the author, you can do it on [Tribute](https://web.tribute.tg/d/TQG). It is voluntary and unlocks nothing.

## По-русски

Об ошибке сообщайте через [форму «Ошибка»](https://github.com/5DHelLD5/dynamic-audio-releases/issues/new?template=bug_report.yml), идеи присылайте через [форму «Идея»](https://github.com/5DHelLD5/dynamic-audio-releases/issues/new?template=feature_request.yml). Писать можно по-русски.
